
Here is the proof idea in its cleanest finite-range form.

Take a graph of bounded degree (\zeta), with Hamiltonian

[
H=\sum_{\langle u v\rangle} h_{uv},
\qquad |h_{uv}|\le J,
]

and let (A) be supported near (x), (B) near (y), with graph distance (r=d(x,y)). Ignore on-site terms; they only rotate local operators and do not spread support.

Use the Dyson expansion

[
A(t)=e^{iHt}Ae^{-iHt}
=\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{(it)^n}{n!}\operatorname{ad}_H^n(A),
]

where

[
\operatorname{ad}_H(A)=[H,A].
]

Now expand the nested commutator:

[
\operatorname{ad}_H^n(A)
========================

\sum_{e_1,\dots,e_n}
\operatorname{ad}*{h*{e_n}}\cdots \operatorname{ad}*{h*{e_1}}(A).
]

A term contributes to ([A(t),B]) only if the sequence of edges (e_1,\dots,e_n) forms a connected path that starts at (x) and reaches (y). Hence no contribution is possible unless

[
n\ge r.
]

Moreover,

[
|\operatorname{ad}*{h*{e_n}}\cdots \operatorname{ad}*{h*{e_1}}(A)|
\le (2J)^n|A|.
]

The number of possible edge-walks of length (n) is bounded by (\zeta^n). Therefore

[
|[A(t),B]|
\le
2|A||B|
\sum_{n\ge r}
\frac{(2J\zeta |t|)^n}{n!}.
]

Now use, for any (\mu>0),

[
\sum_{n\ge r}\frac{a^n}{n!}
\le
e^{-\mu r}\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(ae^\mu)^n}{n!}
===========================================

\exp(-\mu r+a e^\mu).
]

With (a=2J\zeta |t|), this gives

[
|[A(t),B]|
\le
2|A||B|
\exp!\left[-\mu r+2J\zeta e^\mu |t|\right].
]

Equivalently,

[
\boxed{
|[A(t),B]|
\le
2|A||B|
\exp!\left[-\mu\bigl(d(x,y)-v_\mu |t|\bigr)\right]
}
]

with

[
v_\mu=\frac{2J\zeta e^\mu}{\mu}.
]

For extended supports (X,Y), one sums over possible starting and ending sites, giving a prefactor such as (|X||Y|), or a sharper boundary-dependent prefactor in refined versions.

The whole proof is: **nested commutators can only grow support one interaction edge at a time; reaching distance (r) requires at least (r) commutators; the Dyson tail beyond order (r) is exponentially small outside a linear light cone.**
